    - mergeEnv(existing, delta): null/undefined/'' deletes keys; others overwrite; unrelated keys preserved (mirror config.cjs writeEnv merge semantics)
    - isConfigured(env): true iff (SLACK_BOT_TOKEN && SLACK_APP_TOKEN) || TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN (mirror config.cjs:isConfigured)
